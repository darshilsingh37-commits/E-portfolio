ABOUT ME

Hello! I am Darshil Singh, a student of Class X-B at Gayatri Public School. I am a curious learner who enjoys exploring concepts that challenge my thinking and expand my understanding of the world.

Among academic subjects, I have a strong interest in Physics and Chemistry because I enjoy understanding how scientific principles explain real-life phenomena. I like solving logical problems and learning about how things work at a deeper level.

Apart from academics, I enjoy reading manga and psychological thriller novels, as they improve my imagination, analytical thinking, and creativity. I am also interested in swimming, which helps me stay disciplined, active, and focused.

I believe in continuous self-improvement and always try to develop new skills, gain knowledge, and grow both academically and personally. My goal is to become a knowledgeable and capable individual who can use creativity and problem-solving skills to make a positive impact in the future.
